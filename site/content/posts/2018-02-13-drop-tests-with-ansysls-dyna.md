Drop tests with ANSYS/LS-DYNA
Colleagues from the British-Irish CADFEM collected a small presentation on the drop tests simulation in ANSYS with usage of LS-DYNA solver

<!--more-->

From the marketing point everything is not even bad, but when I look at the quality of their FE models, corner of my eye starts to twitch.

In general, I now say the terrible idea for LS-DYNA evangelist. ANSYS Explicit STR product could be a better tool for simplest tasks of drop tests (as for example described in the present), :

<ul>
<li>ANSYS Explicit STR already goes under the license of ANSYS Mechanical Enterprise</li>
<li>can immediately run on 4 CPU cores</li>
<li>works with ANSYS HPC (Pack / Workgroup) licenses for parallelization</li>
<li>does not require long training of specialists</li>
</ul>

So that, ANSYS Explicit STR will be enough in case of simple drop test cases.

You could find original slides here: http://www.cadfemukandireland.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/0930-Ian-turner-Drop-Test.pdf
